# CowBubbles

A fork of [OpenBubbles](https://github.com/OpenBubbles/openbubbles-app) with a rebuilt frontend. Complete with liquid glass, more color, and a music engine that themes the app around you.

## What's different?

- **Music Theming**: Pulls album art through notification and colors app via dominant and sub colors
- **Lyrics**: Timed lyrics with a fullscreen Apple Music-style view (hold the now playing bar to peek, swipe down to go full screen)
- **Color**: so much color
- **Cow** 🐄
