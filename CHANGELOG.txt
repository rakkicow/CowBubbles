CowBubbles
==========

CowBubbles is a fork of OpenBubbles that rebuilds the interface while leaving
the messaging core untouched. It adds a music engine that themes the app
around whatever is playing, synced lyrics with a pull-down sheet, a glass
surface language, and per-conversation colour derived from the handle. Every
Apple-service, sync and message-delivery path is still upstream's work; this
fork only touches the layer above it.

Everything below is relative to upstream commit eed1b63.


MUSIC AND LYRICS
----------------
- The Android media-session listener now forwards a whole track rather than
  only the album art: title, artist, album, playback position, duration,
  transport state and a 128x128 cover.
- Album art crosses the platform channel as raw RGBA instead of a PNG. At that
  size the compressed bytes are no smaller and both ends were paying for the
  codec; the palette can now read the buffer directly.
- A new media-control channel drives whichever session the listener is already
  watching, so play, pause, skip and seek act on the player that is colouring
  the screen rather than on a separate audio session.
- Now-playing chip sits under the conversation header: artwork, title, artist
  and an equaliser that animates while playing and becomes a play mark when
  paused. Tap to play or pause, swipe left for the next track, right for the
  previous one.
- The conversation background is the album art itself. A 28-pixel box-filtered
  copy of the cover is drawn three times under a fragment shader
  (shaders/album_warp.frag), each copy rotating and drifting at its own rate,
  with a centre twist, a saturation lift to undo the blur's greying, a scrim
  toward the ground colour for legibility, and a dither pass so the gradients
  do not band at 8 bits.
- Where the shader will not compile the background falls back to a painter
  that draws the cover at several rotations; a track with no artwork at all
  falls back to drifting colour blobs taken from the palette.
- The warp speeds up briefly each time the lyric line changes, and stops
  entirely while the conversation page is sliding in or out.
- Covers cross-fade over 650ms when the track changes, with the outgoing cover
  still drifting underneath.
- Synced lyrics are fetched from LRCLIB, matched on title, artist, album and
  duration with a fuzzy search as a fallback. Hits are cached for the session;
  misses are cached for ten minutes so a song with no sheet is not re-queried
  on every position report.
- While a song is playing the current lyric line replaces the composer
  placeholder, and the caret is hidden until you start typing so there is no
  cursor sitting beside a lyric.
- Lyric sheet: hold the now-playing chip and a panel peeks open; keep holding
  and pull down about 90 pixels and it commits to full screen. Releasing from
  the peek closes it. Back closes an open sheet before it pops the
  conversation.
- Lines are laid out once and centred on the current one, scrolled with a
  spring that inherits the list's current velocity. The active line swells and
  brightens; lines more than one away go soft with a blur. Tapping any line
  seeks to it, and dragging the list by hand suspends auto-scroll for four
  seconds.
- Instrumental stretches are rendered as three dots that fill left to right
  across the length of the break, then swell and fade into the next line.
  Gaps are inferred from line starts, since LRC carries no line lengths.
- The composer shows the same interval dots during an instrumental stretch,
  and typing dots while a sheet is still loading.
- The full-screen sheet carries its own transport - previous, play/pause, next
  and close - as chrome that fades out after four seconds and returns on a tap.
- Album palette extraction bins colours at five bits per channel, scores bins
  by population weighted toward saturation and mid-lightness, and rejects
  picks that are too close to one already taken. It yields a dominant colour,
  a bubble colour, a most-vivid "pop" colour and a legibility-clamped display
  set used for the equaliser bars.
- Outgoing bubbles take the cover's pop colour while a song is playing.
- Settings > Theming gained a Music section with a live now-playing preview -
  artwork, title, album and moving bars - and the Colors from Media switch
  moved into it from the Advanced Theming section.
- The shader and the lyric type variations are warmed up at app start so the
  first hold does not stall.


INTERFACE
---------
- New design layer under lib/utils/cow holding the shared vocabulary: a
  four-point spacing scale, motion durations and curves (including a spring
  that overshoots and settles), a press-scale wrapper that respects the
  system's reduce-motion setting, and the type ramp.
- Liquid glass surfaces: a translucent fill, a one-pixel rim that runs bright
  along the top edge and dim along the bottom, and a diagonal sheen. Glass
  blurs its backdrop; GlassFill skips the blur, for surfaces that repeat many
  times over - list rows, settings cards - where a backdrop filter per
  instance would cost frames for an identical result.
- Shape language: a superellipse border for circular shapes, a corners-only
  variant for wide rectangles, and a deterministic cow-spot blob whose lobes
  are seeded from a handle so a given contact always gets the same silhouette.
- Conversation list header is a glass panel. Search and compose became round
  glass buttons.
- The condensed title strip that used to fade in over the iOS list as you
  scrolled is no longer shown; the glass header carries the title on its own.
 
- The new-message button is a glass capsule with a "New" label rather than a
  round icon button - on a glass surface the icon alone reads as decoration.
  The camera button is a matching capsule.
- Conversation tiles: larger titles, circular avatars, and unread rows that
  fill with the conversation's own colour and inset themselves with rounded
  corners. Read rows stay flat. Interaction highlighting still wins over the
  fill.
- Pinned tiles: the unread dot became a ring drawn around the avatar in the
  conversation's colour, concentric with the circle.
- Contact avatars are circular with no border by default; callers that overlap
  avatars pass their own.
- Message bubbles lost the tail. Every corner is rounded and the gutter on the
  tail side is kept empty. Incoming bubbles are glass, with the rim painted as
  a stroke along the bubble path - a Border would be clipped on the curves.
- The composer is a glass capsule, as are the attachment row surfaces and the
  send-effect picker.
- The contact suggestion and "share your name and photo" prompts moved out of
  the conversation header into a glass card above the composer, where they no
  longer change the app bar's height.
- Settings on the iOS skin: sections are glass cards, section headings are a
  real title rather than a small grey caption, dividers are a faint etched
  line rather than the theme outline colour, and the Settings page leads with
  a large display title that scrolls under the small one in the bar.
- Setup flow restyled: the welcome card is the first glass surface you see,
  Back is a glass capsule, Next is an accent capsule (the one action that
  moves you on is the one surface that is not glass), and the Apple ID fields
  and 2FA code cells are glass with an accent rim on focus.
- Fullscreen media uses a translucent glass app bar rather than a backdrop
  blur, which would otherwise resample the video every frame.
- Message popup: the emoji picker and the reaction tray are glass.
- Conversation details: media gallery cards are glass, and the add, more and
  error buttons became rounded squares rather than circles.
- Chat creator: selected recipients are glass capsules that keep the service
  tint (it is how you tell an iMessage recipient from an SMS one), and the
  address field is a glass capsule.
- Two variable fonts are bundled: Bricolage Grotesque for display, titles and
  names, Instrument Sans for body, secondary text and labels.
- Repaint boundaries were added around the message list and the pinned-chat
  grid so the animated background does not drag them into a repaint every
  frame.


COLOUR AND THEMES
-----------------
- An OKLCH colour type converts perceptual lightness, chroma and hue to sRGB
  with gamut clipping, and is the basis for every colour the fork generates.
- Per-conversation signature colour: the participant's handle, or the chat
  GUID for a group, is hashed with FNV-1a onto a fixed twelve-step hue wheel
  with lightness and chroma held constant, so all twelve carry the same visual
  weight and no two threads land a few degrees apart. This replaces upstream's
  seven hand-picked gradients, which were selected by a seeded Random from the
  sum of the string's code units.
- The signature drives avatar gradients, the unread fill on list tiles, the
  pinned-tile unread ring, and the outgoing bubble colour when nothing is
  playing.
- Two new stock themes, "CowBubbles light" and "CowBubbles dark", built on a
  brown ground with a pink accent. They are the defaults for a fresh install
  and the fallback that music theming restores to when it is switched off,
  in place of Bright White and OLED Dark.
- Theme presets added after install are now merged into the theme box when
  themes are read, so an existing install picks up new stock themes instead of
  being stuck with whatever shipped at its first launch.
- Music theming reseeds the Material colour scheme from the palette's dominant
  colour rather than quantising the artwork a second time through an image
  provider - which is both correct for the raw-pixel transport and faster.


BRANDING
--------
- Renamed to CowBubbles throughout: window and system-tray titles, setup copy,
  Apple ID and VPN warnings, share sheet subjects, log exports, the about
  panel and the settings pages. The conversation list header reads
  "CowMessages".
- New adaptive launcher icon with a monochrome layer for Android's themed
  icons, and splash screens for light and dark.
- Launcher icon picker in Settings > Theming, offering a cow face or a bubbles
  mark. It works by enabling one activity-alias and disabling the other, in
  that order so the launcher never briefly has no entry at all. MainActivity
  carries no LAUNCHER filter in the cow flavour, so exactly one alias is ever
  enabled.
- README rewritten for the fork, with build instructions, a statement of what
  is upstream's work, and three demo captures under docs/media.


REDACTED MODE
-------------
- The lorem-ipsum generator was replaced with a cow substitution set. Contact
  names become cow puns ("Mootilda Hoofman") or cow-ified television
  characters ("Dwight Moorute"), seeded from the handle so the same person is
  always the same cow.
- Group chats get a herd name ("Bovine Intervention", "Udder Chaos") seeded
  from the chat GUID, instead of the literal string "Group Chat".
- Message text becomes cow riddles and one-line pasture stories, generated to
  the same word count as the real message so bubbles keep their shape, with
  the final word punctuated so a trimmed passage still ends cleanly. Very
  short messages get a short stand-in rather than a truncated sentence.
- Hidden avatars show a cow face rather than contact initials.
- FaceTime overlays, reply bubbles, pinned tile previews and the theme
  selector preview all use the same substitutions, so the whole app tells one
  story rather than several unrelated ones.


FIXES
-----
- Media session callbacks now hold a reference to their controller and read
  the live transport state when metadata changes. Upstream published with a
  null playback state, so every track change reported position 0 and isPlaying
  false - lyrics froze on the first line and the equaliser never moved.
- Platform-channel sends from media callbacks are posted to the main looper.
  MediaController callbacks arrive on a binder thread, and Flutter channel
  calls made off the main thread are dropped with no error at all.
- The "same track, skip the artwork" check is keyed on the track artwork was
  last shipped for, not on the last title seen. Players fill the album-art
  bitmap asynchronously, so the second metadata event for a track - the one
  that actually carries the cover - was being discarded as a duplicate: the
  song changed and the background did not.
- LRCLIB responses are read to completion, and drained on any failure. An
  undrained response leaves its connection half-read in the pool, and after a
  few of those the client stops issuing requests entirely part-way through a
  session.
- Redacted mode no longer applies a message's annotation ranges to the
  substituted text. Those ranges index the real string, the stand-in is a
  different length, and a range extending past the end of a shorter
  replacement raised a RangeError and the message failed to render.
- A faded-out header stops blurring. An Opacity of zero still lets a
  BackdropFilter inside it sample and blur the backdrop, which was the visible
  smear at the top of a scrolled list; the subtree is now hidden outright
  while keeping its size so the scroll extent does not shift.
- The listener re-runs its initialisation when it connects. Media sessions are
  only handed to a connected listener, so the app now picks up whatever is
  already playing the moment notification access is granted, rather than
  waiting for the next track change.
- A republish path lets a cold-started engine ask the platform what is
  playing. The listener connects and publishes at boot, long before Flutter is
  listening, so the app used to come up unthemed until the next song.
- Switching players publishes the new track immediately instead of waiting for
  the next metadata change, which used to leave the previous track's colours
  up until the song after next.
- Clearing the theme is debounced by 2.5 seconds. Players emit brief
  non-playing states while changing tracks and while buffering, and reacting
  to them immediately made the whole theme flicker off and back on between
  songs.
- Playback-state reports are throttled to one every five seconds unless the
  track changed. Players emit one a second or so, and the position is
  interpolated on the Dart side anyway, so the rest were dozens of channel
  round-trips a minute for nothing.
- Chats with no participants and no messages are filtered out of every
  conversation list. They are leftovers of a sync that resolved no
  participants, and listed as "Unnamed chat" over a blank preview with nothing
  to do inside them.
- The composer no longer rebuilds on every keystroke. It now watches only the
  empty/non-empty transition, which is all the placeholder logic needs; the
  per-keystroke rebuild was dropping keyboard input.
- Contact photos decode at the device's pixel ratio with medium filtering,
  instead of a fixed 2x with filtering disabled, which left avatars looking
  soft and blocky.
- Custom chat and user avatars are clipped and cover-fitted directly rather
  than going through CircleAvatar's background image, which was letterboxing
  non-square pictures.


BUILD
-----
- New "cow" product flavour with its own application id
  (com.cowbubbles.messaging), app name, launcher background colour and file
  provider authority, so it installs alongside a stock OpenBubbles build
  rather than replacing it.
- The cow flavour builds arm64 only. The other three ABIs were roughly 500 MB
  of dead weight for a target that is always a phone.
- Distributed builds are signed with a project release key, so the shared APK
  installs alongside a stock OpenBubbles build rather than updating it.
- TTF and OTF assets are stored uncompressed in the APK, so fonts are
  memory-mapped on first use rather than inflated.
- Two variable fonts and one new fragment shader are registered in pubspec.
- Build command:
      flutter build apk --release --flavor cow --target-platform android-arm64


LIMITS
------
- Android arm64 only. Built and tested on a Galaxy S24. The desktop and web
  targets still compile but none of the new interface work was designed for
  them.
- A build from source cannot authenticate with Apple. The fairplay
  certificates and the Absinthe validation code are withheld upstream and ship
  as stubs. That limitation is inherited from OpenBubbles and is not something
  this fork changes.
- SF Pro is not bundled - Apple's licence keeps it off other platforms. It is
  downloaded at runtime from Settings > Theming, the same way the emoji font
  already was, and Bricolage Grotesque stands in until it has loaded.
